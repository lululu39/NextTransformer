# Independent parallel and perpendicular displacement gains

Compare with the [high-scalar-LR zero-initial-velocity ChordRes](https://wandb.ai/yibozhong657-none/nexttransformer-chordres/runs/13fb5703). Start from scratch with seed 42 and seed-43 normal attention/FFN output projections. Preserve 23 layers, width 1,536, FFN width 6,144, full attention, input smear, 12 VE tables, zero initial velocity, scalar position controls, saved BF16 forces and the ordinary LM head. The three velocity coefficients apply to every attention and FFN write. The final unused velocity has fixed coefficients. Two direct learnable gains per consumed velocity add 90 parameters; the 273 Chord control entries share AdamW peak LR 0.0024494897427831783 with zero weight decay.

All three effective velocity gains start at 0.5, recovering the original update. The [method documentation](../../nexttransformer/methods/chordres_velocity_gains/README.md) describes the split and the zero-direction case. Check the fused BF16 writes and all derivatives against an independent FP64 expression on every training GPU, then verify common weights and initial logits against the ordinary high-LR control. Save the gains by depth at each validation; report coefficient medians for VE attention, attention without VE, and FFN in W&B.

The [fixed-shape configuration](../../configs/residual/chordres_velocity_gains.json) uses the same ClimbMix stream, 2,048 context, eight GPUs, per-rank microbatch eight and eight accumulation steps. Run to 8,000 updates (8,388,608,000 tokens) on the original 46,875-update LR schedule and save validation, CORE-22 and the checkpoint. The VE-only fixed-turn experiment was stopped after its 500-step checkpoint. The high-LR control supplies matched points through 4,000.

The production-width eight-GPU preflight passed BF16 position/velocity equality against the baseline at the initial coefficients and the independent forward/backward oracle with distinct coefficients. The two-step training check observed nonzero gradients for each gamma family on its second update. All 306 shared tensors matched exactly at initialization, and first-step loss was 10.39548248.
